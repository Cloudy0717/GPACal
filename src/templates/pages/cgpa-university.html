How {{university_short_name}} CGPA Calculation Works

At {{university_full_name}}, your CGPA is updated at the conclusion of every academic semester. It incorporates all credit-bearing subjects attempted throughout your study program.

Formula:
Cumulative CGPA = Total Quality Points (All Semesters) ÷ Total Credit Hours (All Semesters)
  • Determine total points earned in each semester (GPA × Semester Credits).
  • Sum all semester points to obtain total cumulative quality points.
  • Divide cumulative quality points by total cumulative credit hours attempted.
