How WAM is Calculated

WAM is calculated by multiplying the raw marks earned in each course by the course's credit hours and year level weighting, summing the result, and dividing by the total weighted credit hours:

WAM = ∑ (Mark × Credit Hours × Year Weight) ÷ ∑ (Credit Hours × Year Weight)
