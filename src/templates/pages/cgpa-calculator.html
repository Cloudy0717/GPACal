How CGPA is Calculated

Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) represents your overall academic performance across all completed semesters. It is calculated by dividing total quality points earned across all semesters by total credit hours completed:

CGPA = ∑ (All Semester Total Points) ÷ ∑ (All Semester Total Credits)
