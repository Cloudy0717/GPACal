How to calculate {{university_short_name}} WAM

At {{university_full_name}}, your Weighted Average Mark (WAM) is the average of the actual marks you achieve in all completed units, weighted by the credit point value of each unit and its respective year-level weighting.

Formula:
WAM = Σ(Mark × Credit Points × Year-Level Weight) ÷ Σ(Credit Points × Year-Level Weight)
