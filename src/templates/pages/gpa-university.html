How to calculate {{university_short_name}} GPA

At {{university_full_name}}, your Grade Point Average (GPA) is computed at the end of each semester. Each grade awarded corresponds to a fixed grade point value as specified in the university's academic regulations.

Formula:
Semester GPA = Total Grade Points Earned ÷ Total Credit Hours Attempted
  • Multiply the grade points earned by the credit units of each course.
  • Add the products together to get the total grade points earned.
  • Divide total grade points by the total credit units taken in the semester.
