How Semester GPA is Calculated

Semester GPA is calculated by multiplying the grade points earned in each course by the course's credit hours, summing the result, and dividing by the total credit hours attempted in that semester:

GPA = ∑ (Course Grade Points × Credit Hours) ÷ Total Credit Hours
